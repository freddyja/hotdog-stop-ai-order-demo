1. **Combo drink:** the site just says "drink". The app only offers Water or YooHoo, defaults to Water, and labels the line "drink choice assumed".
2. **Cheese and chili charges:** both appear in the free NY topping list and the paid $2 list. The app charges +$2 for cheese, chili, and peppers & onions, and treats mustard, ketchup and cooked red onions as free.
3. **Which toppings go on which items:** the app uses the NY set for kielbasa, Nathan, Italian sausage and their combos. Chicago dogs also get the paid toppings. None of this is stated on the site.
4. **Single Italian Beef Sandwich:** it's only listed as a $16 combo. The app shows "price not listed" and leaves it out of the total.
5. Corn dog and extras get no topping options.
6. No tax, sizes, or Dog House Fries toppings are listed, so the totals leave out tax.
7. The "Nathan Jumbo" combo was renamed "Nathan Jumbo Combo" so it doesn't clash with the single item of the same name.
